SoapySDR (optional multi-device facade)
=======================================

NeuroSDR does not bundle SoapySDR.dll. When PothosSDR (or another SoapySDR
install) is present, NeuroSDR loads SoapySDR.dll from common paths such as:

  %ProgramFiles%\PothosSDR\bin\SoapySDR.dll
  %SOAPY_SDR_ROOT%\bin\SoapySDR.dll

Installed Soapy modules then expose LimeSDR, PlutoSDR, BladeRF, USRP,
AirspyHF (if no native DLL), and other drivers in the SOURCE list.

Native adapters already cover RTL-SDR, HackRF, SDRplay, and Airspy; those
Soapy drivers are skipped to avoid duplicate entries.

Install: https://github.com/pothosware/PothosSDR/wiki
Probe:   NeuroSDR.exe --probe-soapy
