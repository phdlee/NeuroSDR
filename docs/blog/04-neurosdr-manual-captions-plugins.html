NeuroSDR user manual (2) — NeuroCaption AI & AF plugins

This post covers the features that make NeuroSDR feel different on shortwave and utility listening. Author: KD8CEC.

1. AF plugin host (mental model)

AF plugins receive demodulated audio from a chosen VFO (main or a Sub VFO). Each SCENE can own multiple plugin instances — for example three FT8 decoders on three dial frequencies — without juggling separate programs.

  1. Open SET → AF Plugins.
  2. Add a row, choose the plugin type, set the VFO target, APPLY.
  3. Use the AF tab strip on the right of the main window for that decoder’s UI.

3. Shortwave helpers + AI language scan

EiBi / shortwave scheduler style plugins help you browse broadcast schedules, filter by language or country, and jump the VFO. AI scan can walk candidates and look for real speech / language — useful when you do not want to hand-spin every carrier.

5. RNNoise and active CW filter

RNNoise is an AI voice extractor / noise suppressor on the AF path. Blend dry vs processed until speech is clear without sounding hollow. The active CW filter boosts Morse-shaped energy for listening or external decoders.

6. Dual audio to other programs

Enable OUT2 in SET → Audio, point it at VB-CABLE (or similar), and feed WSJT-X / FLDIGI / DeepCW while you keep speakers on OUT1.

Video: Dual audio + VB-CABLE (03:12)
Video: WSJT-X external audio (04:18)
Caption quality follows SNR and mode. AM shortwave with music beds will hallucinate more than clean SSB speech. Prefer Base/Small ONNX or Groq when Tiny is too optimistic.
