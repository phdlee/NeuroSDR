NeuroSDR user manual (2) — NeuroCaption and AF plugins

Plugins attach to demodulated audio (or IQ) from your receiver. They are most powerful on local hardware with a stable sample stream; they also work on Virtual Kiwi/WebSDR when you use that secondary path. Author: KD8CEC.

1. Mental model

  1. SET → AF Plugins → add a row → choose type → assign VFO (main or a Sub VFO) → APPLY.
  2. Each SCENE owns its plugin instance list.
  3. Decoder UIs appear as tabs on the right (FT8, CW, Caption, …).

3. Shortwave schedule + AI scan

EiBi / shortwave scheduler style plugins browse broadcast schedules, filter by language/country, and jump the VFO. AI scan can look for live speech/language while you leave the radio on a hardware or remote HF path.

5. RNNoise, AF bandpass, active CW filter

RNNoise extracts voice from noisy AM/SSB/FM audio — blend dry vs processed. AF bandpass cuts rumble/hiss. Active CW filter boosts Morse-shaped energy for ears or external decoders.
