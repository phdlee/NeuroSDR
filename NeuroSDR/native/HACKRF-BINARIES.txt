HackRF Windows native binaries
================================

Files:
- win-x64/hackrf.dll
- win-x86/hackrf.dll

Built locally with Microsoft Visual C++ from the official Great Scott Gadgets
HackRF v2026.01.3 source release:
https://github.com/greatscottgadgets/hackrf/tree/v2026.01.3

The DLLs link statically to pthreads4w and dynamically to libusb-1.0.dll.
The accompanying x64/x86 libusb DLLs are from the official libusb v1.0.30
Windows binary release:
https://github.com/libusb/libusb/releases/tag/v1.0.30

See HACKRF-COPYING.txt, PTHREADS4W-LICENSE.txt and LIBUSB-COPYING.txt.
No HackRF firmware image is bundled or written by NeuroSDR.
